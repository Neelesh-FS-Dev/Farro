# FARRO — A TABLE OF STORIES
## Premium Culinary Website · Codevirel Studio

## 01. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Design, develop, test, refine and deliver an exceptionally premium, original, media-rich, responsive and production-ready React website for FARRO, a contemporary restaurant and cocktail bar located in Koregaon Park, Pune, India.

This is an independent creative website proposal by Codevirel Studio.

The objective is to create a website that captures the emotional, cultural, culinary and sensory identity of Farro.

Do not build a generic restaurant template.

Do not make the website look like an AI-generated landing page.

Do not create a conventional hospitality website consisting of a full-screen video, three feature cards, generic menu cards and a booking form.

Create a website that feels like a beautifully designed independent culinary journal translated into an immersive digital dining experience.

The website should combine:

- Premium restaurant art direction.
- Authentic culinary storytelling.
- Contemporary Indian cultural references.
- Editorial typography.
- Tactile visual design.
- Authentic photography.
- Cinematic food and kitchen footage.
- Meaningful motion design.
- Chef-led storytelling.
- Accessible restaurant information.
- A convenient reservation journey.
- Professional frontend engineering.

The experience should feel warm, thoughtful, contemporary, expressive, intimate and memorable.

It must be suitable for presenting to Farro's management as a high-quality private website concept.

## 03. CREATIVE CONCEPT

### A TABLE OF STORIES

The central creative idea is that food carries memories, cultural influences, personal journeys and shared traditions.

Farro should be presented as a place where those stories meet around the table.

Potential creative headline:

Every plate begins somewhere.

Supporting positioning:

Inspired by culture, shaped by journeys, and brought together around the table.

These are proposed website copy concepts, not official quotations.

The digital experience should unfold in culinary chapters:

01. The invitation.

02. The philosophy.

03. The kitchen.

04. The food.

05. The bar.

06. The people.

07. The shared table.

08. The return to Farro.

The website should have the intimacy of a culinary journal and the usability of a professionally built modern hospitality website.

Do not make the design look like a historical manuscript, vintage scrapbook or artificial old-world restaurant.

Use contemporary editorial composition with restrained tactile details.
